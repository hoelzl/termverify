- **Terminal epoch evidence failures are structured at the boundary.** A chunk that cannot be encoded as strict UTF-8 (only producible by an injected child port; the shipped bindings decode with replacement) now fails the epoch immediately with `{"during": "read", "encoding": "utf-8"}` instead of surviving into an observation the transcript codec must reject at serialization time, which previously lost the whole transcript. Additionally, when the terminal binding cannot be closed after the child exited, the already-built observation (including the exit evidence) is now retained on the failed run instead of being discarded.
