- Fixed the ConPTY adapter's terminal receipt claiming `tier="os"` for geometries the pseudoconsole never adopted: `ConptyChild.spawn` and `ConptyChild.resize` now refuse requests that cannot survive the console's signed 16-bit `COORD` members (silent truncation to a smaller size, creation failure or silent no-op, or child death at console attach) plus the resize-only kill band at an axis of 32767 (measured: with an otherwise adoptable geometry the client dies while the resize reports success), and measure the adopted size of every remaining geometry with a probe child's read-back, cached per process — a spawn the console cannot adopt exactly fails the start, and a resize it cannot adopt exactly fails the run, with a structured geometry error naming the requested and adopted sizes instead of running the subject at a size the receipt never recorded ([#228](https://github.com/hoelzl/termverify/issues/228)).
