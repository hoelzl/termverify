- **An observed end-of-stream survives a close in the deferred-delivery window.** Both terminal bindings now latch a genuinely observed end-of-stream on the read path: a read owed the deferred raise after a close reports `EndOfStream` instead of `Closed`, without re-capturing the exit record and without bypassing the single-flight guard; `write` keeps the closed-binding contract. A run whose end-of-stream was observed before the deadline-driven close reached its reader therefore still finishes with its exit record — including when the observation lands in the expire→wake window, which the adapter cannot distinguish from an observation that preceded the deadline. An expired deadline otherwise remains a failed epoch, and any exit captured by the deadline-driven close is retained as evidence on that failure instead of vanishing.
